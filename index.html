04 Private beta · invite-only

Pusula

An AI-assisted student productivity application that I am developing and refining. It brings a student’s timetable, deadlines, focus sessions and goals into one calm place.

The problem: student life is split across timetables, deadline trackers, focus timers and notes, so planning a realistic week takes effort. Pusula keeps these together, and its planning assistant proposes changes that the user approves before anything is added.

My role: a solo project. I built it myself with substantial help from AI coding tools, and I continue to test and refine it.

  • Vercel
