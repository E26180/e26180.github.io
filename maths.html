Why binary matters

Computers represent information using two states, commonly written as 0 and 1. Binary therefore underpins digital logic, data storage, machine instructions and the way values are represented in memory.

This small project connects that concept to a practical interaction: validating a decimal input, converting it to base 2 and presenting the result clearly.
